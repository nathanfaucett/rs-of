## Ownership decision

Management owns Devices and endpoint IDs, including bindings, User ownership, approval/enrollment, transfer/revocation, restrictions and selections. IdP owns Users, credentials, Applications, OAuth Clients and token-signing trust; Storage owns resources and consumes current Management endpoint authorization without its own Device or endpoint key. Transport possession, Device approval, OAuth Principal and signer are distinct. An approved endpoint need not have its own IdP signer. Topology creates no extra Node/Device, copied endpoint key or per-service enrollment. Receipt 29's multi-endpoint/waiting-for-decision proposal is superseded by this decision and the implemented cutover.
